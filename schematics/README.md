# schematics

2D cross-sections (XZ) and plan views (XY) of the habitat design, stage 2 of the workflow.

Convention (same as parameters/parameters.json): metres, origin at the shoreline at mean sea level,
+X offshore, +Y alongshore, Z elevation (depth negative). Zones Z1-Z4 and rules R01-R14 come from
parameters/parameters.csv, so a schematic and the Rhino model share one set of numbers.

Suggested files: `<site_id>_section.svg`, `<site_id>_plan.svg`, `module_20x40_plan.svg`, plus the CSV/JSON
they were drawn from.
