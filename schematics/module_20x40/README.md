# module_20x40: lumpfish nest-reef module (DRAFT, paused 2026-11-25)

> Paused at Jesse's request: substrates, flora and fauna need the same evidence work as boulders before any Rhino build. Do not extend.

Generated by `scripts/schematics/module_layout.py` from `parameters/parameters.csv`. Re-run with `.\run.ps1 module` after changing parameters; the Rhino model (run `scripts/rhino/rhino_build_module.py` inside Rhino and pick `module.json`) is built from the same file, so schematic and 3D always match.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| module.json | every object: boulders (x, y, z_base, diameter, height, layer), nest clusters (crevice axis, egg-mass point, edge distance), cobble apron, sand lanes, seabed profile, rule checks |
| plan.svg | plan, offshore up |
| section.svg | cross-shore section from the shoreline through zones Z1–Z3 (5× vertical exaggeration) |
| section_module.svg | true-scale slice through one row of nest clusters |

Design logic: three reef patches (≈12 × 16 m) separated by two 2 m sand lanes, so every nest is within 10 m of a hard/soft edge (R15, Bembridge). Each nest cluster is 4 boulders of 0.8–1.2 m (R03, R04) in two rows around a 0.2 m crevice (R05) turned across the alongshore current (R10), clusters ~3.5 m apart (R06). Filler boulders bring reef cover to R08 (0.3–0.5). Cobble apron 0.3 m thick with 2 m margin (R09). Module sits at −6 to −7.3 m (Z3) on the R02 slope, with ≥ 3 m clearance to the surface (R07b).

Open design questions: Danish nests may sit as shallow as 1–2 m (P01 updated), so a shallower variant (`--z0 -2`) may be worth drawing; ice and wave exposure at that depth need checking.
