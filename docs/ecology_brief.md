Headline: Danish lumpfish nest at about 5-10 m on stable boulders with crevices, spawn Feb-May at 4-8 °C,
need a weedy 0-2 m nursery nearby for juveniles in May-Aug, and prefer salty bottom water (north of Drogden
in the Øresund). Nest geometry (boulder size, crevice size, spacing) has no measured values in the
literature read; those rules are inferred and flagged low confidence until calibrated on real nest sites.
