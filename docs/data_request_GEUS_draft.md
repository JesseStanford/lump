# Draft data request to GEUS (for Jesse to edit and send)

**Subject:** Request for multibeam and sidescan grids, Gilleleje Flak (GEUS report 2016/51), for a lumpfish habitat design study

Dear GEUS marine data team,

I am working on a design study for shallow-water spawning habitat for lumpfish (*Cyclopterus lumpus*) on the North Zealand and Øresund coast. Spawning lumpfish have been recorded at Helsingør (Jansson et al. 2023, PLoS ONE), and Gilleleje Flak is the nearest mapped stone reef at comparable depths (4.6–10 m).

I would like to ask whether the following from Skar et al. (2016), *Investigation at Gilleleje Flak and Tragten in preparation for reef restoration* (GEUS Report 2016/51), can be shared for non-commercial research use:

1. Gridded swath bathymetry (EdgeTech 6205), at the finest resolution available (ideally 0.5 m or finer), as GeoTIFF or XYZ.
2. Sidescan mosaics (550 kHz and/or 230 kHz) as georeferenced GeoTIFF.
3. The interpreted substrate polygons (substrate classes 1–4), with any ground-truth points from diver or video observations.

The data would be used to measure boulder sizes, spacing and slope on a natural reef, so that the design rules for constructed nest reefs can be calibrated. I am happy to credit GEUS as you prefer and to share the derived results.

If other Øresund or North Zealand reef surveys at 0–15 m are available, I would be grateful for pointers to those as well.

Kind regards,
Jesse
