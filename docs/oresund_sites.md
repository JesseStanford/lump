# Øresund candidate reef sites: what the data say (2026-11-25)

Patches come from `shortlist` (box 12.3–12.95 E, 55.45–56.15 N): screen score 3 (coarse substrate on glacial till, 0–10 m) or mapped reef ≤ 10 m. Species are GBIF records within 2 km of the patch edge, from 2000 onwards.

Sources: `emodnet_substrate250k`, `emodnet_seafloorgeology`, `emodnet_dtm2024`, `helcom_reefs1170`, `helcom_zostera`, `gbif_occurrences` (spring sightings), and `gbif_site_species`: GBIF.org (25 November 2026) GBIF Occurrence Download https://doi.org/10.15468/dl.anwzt9 (29,319 records). The first download that day (10.15468/dl.c935zs) was wrong (a parent-taxon fallback pulled in all arthropods). Do not cite it.

No GBIF record means nobody reported one, not that the species is absent. Swedish monitoring is denser than Danish here.

## For the knowledge base
- **Grazing sea urchins are almost absent**: 1 record (Strongylocentrotus droebachiensis, #13, 2015) across all 43 patches. Most urchin records are burrowing heart urchins. Kelp loss to urchin grazing (A7) is unlikely to matter in Øresund. The likely reason is low surface salinity, an inference not tested here.
- **Goldsinny and corkwing wrasse** (A1, likely egg predators) occur at most patches with reef fish records: #3, #5, #36, #37, #44, #47, #50, #54, #56.
- **Kelp** (Saccharina latissima, Laminaria digitata) is recorded at #1, #2, #3, #13, #36, #39–#43, #47 and #50: the northern patches and the Swedish coast.
