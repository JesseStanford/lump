# dk_gilleleje_flak: Gilleleje Flak and Tragten (GEUS report 2016/51)

Source: Skar et al. 2016, *Investigation at Gilleleje Flak and Tragten in preparation for reef restoration*, GEUS Rapport 2016/51 (PDF in project files: 32472_GEUS-R_2016_51_opt.pdf). Citation key `geus2016gilleleje`.

## What it gives us
- **Danish reef geometry:** moraine plateau ~6.8 km², shallowest part 4.6 m, flat to 18 m 5 km NW of Gilleleje harbour; boulder reef with sandy patches and "a few cavernous elements".
- **Stone sizes:** common stones "tens of centimetres to over 1 m"; recommended for restoration. Wave-driven bed shear moves sand and gravel up to ~2.5 cm at 25 m/s winds, so placed stones must be well above that.
- **Substrate classes (SVANA 2012), by stone cover >10 cm:** 1 soft sediment; 2 <10% stones; 3 10–25%; 4 >25% (stone reef). Useful as the Danish target scale for our hard-fraction metric.
- **Restoration designs:** PR1 at ~10 m depth, 18 cones each 4 m high; PR2 at ~15 m, a 600 m submerged stone dam. Direct precedents for our 20 × 40 m module.
- **Biology:** macroalgae cover stones at all depths, most species above the thermocline (~11–12 m); sugar kelp (*Laminaria saccharina*) most abundant <10 m (up to 35% cover). Fish highest in abundance in shallow water, highest in diversity >10 m and on stone reefs.
- **Tide:** M2 range ~0.4–0.5 m, so Danish sites have almost no intertidal; a nest at Bembridge's "low intertidal" position corresponds to roughly 1–2 m depth here.

## What it does not give us
- No lumpfish observed in the dives or transects (survey season not in the spawning window).
- No gridded multibeam data in the report; only maps. The bathymetry and side-scan would need a data request to GEUS (see docs/data_request_GEUS_draft.md).
